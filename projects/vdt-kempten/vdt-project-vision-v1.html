Concept Note · EU-Ready · v1.0

Proof Gap in Digital Transformation

A joint research initiative between IDT Kempten and the PHO Framework (within the WINDI system)
1. Research Problem

Digital transformation frameworks — in learning, onboarding, and organizational change — operate under a structural blind spot: they can document process design, but not process proof.

Current systems measure adoption perception, training completion rates, and self-reported compliance. What they cannot measure is: who made which decision, when, under which authorization, and whether the agreed process was actually followed — not just recorded.

We define this as the Proof Gap: the distance between documented transformation and verifiable transformation.

2. Research Question
"How can digital transformation and organizational learning processes be made independently auditable and cryptographically verifiable — not only documented — using proof-of-oversight mechanisms?"

Sub-questions: What constitutes valid proof of human decision in a learning process? How can PHO (Proof of Human Oversight) be operationalized in onboarding? What is the minimum viable proof layer for EU AI Act Article 14 compliance?

3. Methodology

Empirical base: 16+ KMU pilot companies from the LeBi Interreg project (Vorarlberg, Ostschweiz, Süddeutschland), Q1 2026 – Q4 2028.

Intervention: Integration of a PHO layer into LeBi's onboarding toolbox. Each critical decision step is anchored to an immutable ledger record: actor DID, timestamp, authorization level, process deviation (if any).

Measurement: Comparative analysis of (a) documented process vs (b) proven process execution. Gap quantification. Human oversight coverage score (SGE).

Academic: Peer-reviewed paper — "From Digital Transformation to Verifiable Transformation". Framework definition. Empirical dataset.

Practical: Replicable PHO-onboarding model. Open methodology for KMUs. Certification pathway aligned with EU AI Act Art. 14.

Societal: First empirical evidence base for proof-of-oversight in organizational learning in German-speaking Europe.

5. EU Alignment

This initiative directly addresses EU AI Act Article 14 (Human Oversight) and GDPR Art. 22 requirements for meaningful human involvement in automated systems. The PHO Framework was designed as a constitutional primitive, not a compliance patch.
