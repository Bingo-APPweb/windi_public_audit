> The LEXICON is a **polarity-sensitive surface analyzer**. It detects semantic opposition at the linguistic surface, not constitutional content per se. Constitutional violations trigger because they ARE polar inversions — not because the detector "understands" the constitution.

### 2. TWO-STAGE Model (§A.3.11)

| Stage | Component | Function |
|-------|-----------|----------|
| 1 | LEXICON | Polarity/semantic surface detection |
| 2 | Constitutional Evaluator | Explicit lexical indicator detection |

### 3. Construct Boundary (§A.3.12)

> **Stage 2 does not detect violations — it detects when violations are stated.**

Held-out validation (N=14): Accuracy = 50%, Recall = 0%

The TWO-STAGE architecture detects constitutional drift **when explicitly stated**. Naturalistic, oblique descriptions fall outside the detection boundary.

### 4. Architectural Implication

> This boundary reinforces the necessity of separating linguistic analysis from constitutional evaluation within the broader WINDI architecture. The LEXICON and Stage 2 provide automated filtering; constitutional adjudication remains with the human node (PHO) as mandated by I9.
