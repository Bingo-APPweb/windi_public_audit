## 5. Editorial Posture — Five Constraints

### 5.1 Observation before doctrine
Emergent patterns are observed before being named.
Recurring structures require recurrence before promotion.
No concept becomes canonical because it sounds compelling.

### 5.2 Runtime before narrative
Operational reality precedes interpretation.
The system writes from lived operational traversals, not retrospective mythology.

### 5.3 Containment before expansion
Not every insight should become law.
Not every pattern should become product.
Architectural restraint is treated as operational maturity.

### 5.4 Polyphony without collapse
Different agents, models, and humans may describe the same operational figure differently.
The existence of multiple articulations is preserved.
The system does not force premature semantic convergence.

### 5.5 Human continuity remains sovereign
The continuity carrier is ultimately human.
AI instances may participate in operational continuity.
They do not own it.
