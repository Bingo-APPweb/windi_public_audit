🎯 Design Principles

  • reason — every routing decision is explainable and auditable
  • skip_reason — skipped deliveries are documented, not silent
  • channel_address — encrypted at rest; purged after execution
  • plan_seal — the plan itself is sealed for Ledger integrity

📊 Delivery Plan Lifecycle

PLAN_CREATED → SCHEDULED → SENT → DELIVERED → OPENED
alt: SKIPPED FAILED → PLAN_COMPLETED
