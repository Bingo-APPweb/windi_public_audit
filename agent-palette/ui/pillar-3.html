📋 1. Abstract — Pott as Trust Federation

This document specifies the Pott: the federated governance layer that enables creators to form distribution collectives while preserving individual sovereignty.

A Pott is not a platform. It is:

  • A trust federation — creators voluntarily joining for mutual benefit
  • A discovery network — cross-promotion without algorithmic manipulation
  • A revenue collective — shared audience, transparent splits
  • A governance contract — rules encoded, disputes resolved

🐉 Core Definition

A Pott is a democratic alternative to platforms: creators get network effects of scale while retaining sovereignty of their Vault.

This document covers roles, revenue protocol, charter structure, data boundaries, dispute resolution, technical integration, and the economic model that makes federation sustainable.

💡 The Federation Principle

"Alone, a creator is sovereign. Together, creators are unstoppable. The Pott is the architecture of collective sovereignty."

⚡ 2. The Problem Potts Solve

Individual creators face a fundamental Scale Paradox:

2.1 The Scale Paradox

⚠️ The Platform Trap

Platforms grow by aggregating creators. Once aggregated, they extract: 30-55% revenue cuts, algorithmic control, data ownership, policy unilateralism. Scale becomes a prison.

👥 3. Roles in the Pott Ecosystem

Three distinct roles with clear responsibilities and rights:

🏛️
Creator (Vault Operator)
The sovereign content owner. Operates a Vault, produces content, retains full ownership.
  • Chooses which Potts to join (can join multiple)
  • Sets content visibility per Pott
  • Receives 85% of subscription revenue attributed to their content
  • Can exit any Pott at any time with full content/data
  • Votes on Charter amendments (1 creator = 1 vote)
🔮
Curator (Pott Operator)
The federation organizer. Creates the Pott, recruits creators, manages curation.
  • Defines Pott Charter (rules, focus, revenue split)
  • Curates which creators are featured
  • Receives 15% curation fee from attributed revenue
  • Handles first-tier dispute resolution
  • Can be a creator themselves (common case)
👁️
Subscriber
The audience member. Subscribes to Potts or individual creators.
  • Single subscription = access to all Pott creators
  • View history determines revenue attribution
  • DID-based identity (privacy-preserving)
  • Can subscribe to multiple Potts
  • No lock-in: cancel anytime

🔄 Role Fluidity

A single person can be all three: Creator of their own content, Curator of a Pott, Subscriber to other Potts. The system is composable.

💰 4. The 85/15 Revenue Protocol

Revenue flows transparently from subscriber to creator, with curation fee to the Pott operator.

4.1 Attribution Mechanics

When a subscriber pays for a Pott subscription, revenue is attributed by watch time:

1

WATCH TIME TRACKING

Each creator's Vault logs watch events (content_id, duration, timestamp). Aggregated daily.

2

ATTRIBUTION CALCULATION

Subscriber's monthly watch time summed per creator. Percentage of total = attribution weight.

3

REVENUE SPLIT

Subscription fee × attribution weight × 0.85 = creator payout. Remaining 15% to Curator.

4

MONTHLY SETTLEMENT

Payouts processed monthly. Creator receives direct deposit. Full transparency via Ledger.

5.2 Charter Amendments

Any member can propose a Charter amendment. Process:

  1. Proposal submitted with rationale
  2. 14-day discussion period
  3. Vote (1 creator = 1 vote, weighted by tenure optional)
  4. Passes if ≥67% approve and ≥50% quorum
  5. New Charter version published to all Vaults

⚖️ No Unilateral Changes

Unlike platforms, the Curator cannot change rules unilaterally. Revenue splits, content policies, and exit terms are locked until member vote approves changes.

🔒 6. Data Boundaries — Privacy by Architecture

The Pott protocol enforces strict data boundaries. Privacy is not a policy; it is architecture.

6.2 What the Curator Can See

  • Total subscriber count (not identities)
  • Aggregate watch time per creator (not per subscriber)
  • Revenue attribution percentages
  • Content metadata (titles, categories, publish dates)

6.3 What the Curator Cannot See

  • Individual subscriber identities or DIDs
  • Per-subscriber watch history
  • Creator's other Pott memberships
  • Creator's direct subscriber revenue (outside Pott)
  • Content files (only metadata + thumbnail)

🛡️ GDPR by Design

The architecture ensures GDPR compliance: personal data stays in the Vault, only anonymized aggregates flow to Pott. No consent forms needed — data simply doesn't leave.

⚖️ 7. Dispute Resolution

Disputes are inevitable in any collective. The Pott protocol provides a three-tier escalation system.

7.1 Three-Tier Escalation

T1

CURATOR MEDIATION

First stop. Curator reviews dispute, proposes resolution. Most disputes (80%+) resolve here. Response within 7 days. Non-binding recommendation.

T2

MEMBER ARBITRATION

If T1 fails, 3 random members form arbitration panel. Review evidence, hear both sides. Binding decision within 14 days. Simple majority.

T3

WINDI ARBITRATION

Final appeal. WINDI neutral party reviews case. Binding decision within 30 days. Reserved for Charter violations or revenue disputes >€1000. Fee: €50 (refunded if appellant wins).

🐉 12. Closing — Federation as Democracy of Distribution

The Pott is not a product. It is a political architecture.

  • Platforms are monarchies — one entity makes all rules
  • Potts are federations — members govern themselves
  • Platforms extract rent — value flows to shareholders
  • Potts distribute dividends — value flows to creators

💡 The Political Economy of Distribution

Every distribution system encodes power relations. Platforms encode extraction. Potts encode cooperation. The .jmpg protocol makes cooperation technically enforceable.

The question is not "how do we compete with platforms?" The question is: "how do we make platforms irrelevant?"

The answer is federation:

  • Creators keep their sovereignty (Vaults)
  • Creators gain network effects (Potts)
  • Curators earn fairly for real value (15%)
  • Subscribers get better content, better prices
  • Everyone can exit at any time
