🚀 2. The 7-Day Sovereign Launch

A step-by-step operational plan to go from zero sovereign presence to a fully functional .jmpg distribution channel in seven days. Each day has one primary objective and one deliverable.

DAY 1
Audit Your Existing Network
Objective: Map every distribution channel you already have and quantify its reach.

Open your phone. Count your WhatsApp contacts (not groups — individual contacts who know you). Count your WhatsApp broadcast lists. Count your Telegram subscribers. Count your email newsletter subscribers. Count your Instagram followers.

Write down the total. This is your Sovereign Reach Number. For most creators with any online presence, this number is between 500 and 50,000. This is not a vanity metric; it is a real delivery network where every message arrives.

Deliverable: A written inventory of all channels with reach numbers. Highlight the channels you own.
DAY 2
Activate Your Vault
Objective: Set up your sovereign Vault and verify it works.

Choose your deployment mode:

  • Self-hosted: Rent a VPS from Strato, Hetzner, or any European provider (€4–8/month), install the WINDI Vault runtime, configure your domain.
  • VaaS: Subscribe to a SEED plan (€15/month) — Vault provisioned automatically in under 5 minutes with subdomain or custom domain.

Verify by accessing https://yourdomain.com/health. You should see status: HEALTHY.

Deliverable: A live Vault with working domain, SSL certificate, and passing health check.
DAY 3
Create Your First .jmpg
Objective: Record, upload, and package your first sovereign video.

Record a video. It does not need to be perfect. Introduce yourself, explain what your channel will offer, and give viewers a reason to come back. Keep it between 2 and 10 minutes.

Upload to your Vault. The JMPG Trigger will automatically:

  • Extract the first 30 seconds as the onboard segment
  • Transcode the full video into adaptive HLS streams
  • Assemble the .jmpg binary and seal it with SHA-256
  • Register it in your Ledger

Total processing time: 5–10 minutes. Download and test on your phone.

Deliverable: One .jmpg file, sealed in your Ledger, streamable from your Vault.
DAY 4
Distribute to Your Inner Circle
Objective: Send your .jmpg to 20–50 trusted contacts and collect feedback.

Select your most engaged contacts: friends, family, loyal followers, fellow creators. Send them your .jmpg via WhatsApp (as a document attachment), Telegram, or email.

Include a personal message explaining what you are doing. Ask: did it play immediately? Was the transition to streaming smooth? Would you share this with someone?

Deliverable: Feedback from 20+ contacts confirming the .jmpg experience works.
DAY 5
Build Your Distribution Routine
Objective: Design a sustainable publishing schedule and channel strategy.

Define your publishing frequency (weekly is ideal for most creators). Assign channels to content types:

  • WhatsApp broadcasts: High-value drops (new episode, special announcement)
  • Telegram channels: Regular updates and community interaction
  • Email newsletters: Weekly digests with multiple .jmpg links
  • Social media: Teasers that drive traffic to sovereign channels
Deliverable: A written publishing calendar with channel assignments for the next 4 weeks.
DAY 6
Announce Your Sovereign Channel
Objective: Public launch to your full contact network.

Send a broadcast message to your entire sovereign network. The message should contain:

  • Your first .jmpg as an attachment or link
  • A clear explanation: "I'm launching my own independent video channel — no YouTube, no algorithm, just me directly to you"
  • An invitation to share the .jmpg with anyone interested
  • If applicable, information about your subscription model
Deliverable: Public announcement sent to your full network. First .jmpg distributed at scale.
DAY 7
Measure, Learn, Iterate
Objective: Analyze your first distribution cycle and optimize.

Check your Vault's analytics dashboard:

  • How many times was the .jmpg opened?
  • How many viewers progressed from onboard to full stream?
  • Which distribution channel drove the most views?
  • How many people shared the .jmpg with others?

Compare these numbers with your platform metrics. If your WhatsApp distribution generated more actual views than a typical YouTube upload, you have empirical proof that sovereign distribution works.

Deliverable: A one-page analysis comparing sovereign distribution metrics to your previous platform metrics.

3.5 Social Media: The Migration Funnel

Existing social media accounts (Instagram, TikTok, YouTube) are not discarded; they are repurposed as migration funnels.

  • Post 15-second teasers from the .jmpg onboard segment
  • Call to action: "Watch the full video on my sovereign channel — link in bio"
  • Use Stories and Reels to drive traffic to WhatsApp/Telegram
  • Never upload your full content to a platform

🔄 The Migration Goal

Every follower you move from a platform to a direct channel is a follower you can never lose to an algorithm change. That 10% at 70–95% open rate delivers more actual engagement than the full 100% at 5–10% platform notification rate.

💰 4. Monetization Strategies

Sovereign creators have full control over their monetization model. No platform dictating CPM rates, no algorithm throttling reach, and no 45% revenue share.

4.1 Direct Subscription

The most predictable and sustainable model. Subscribers pay a monthly fee (€3–25) for access to gated .jmpg streams. The 30-second onboard is always free (it serves as the marketing asset). The creator receives 100% of subscription revenue (minus payment processor fees of 1.5–3%).

4.2 Per-Episode Access

For creators who produce irregular or high-value content (masterclasses, investigative reports, premium tutorials). Typical pricing: €1–5 per episode. The Vault's Gatekeeper supports per-content access tokens.

4.3 Community Support (Patronage)

For creators who want to keep all content free but accept voluntary support. The .jmpg player displays a support button after the onboard. Works well for community journalism, educational content, and open-source creators.

4.4 Bundled Courses and Series

Package multiple .jmpg episodes into a course with a single purchase price (€20–200). The Gatekeeper supports series-level access: one payment unlocks all .jmpg in the series.

📈 5. Growth Strategies: From Hundreds to Thousands

5.1 The Sovereign Referral Loop

The .jmpg file is inherently viral: any recipient can share it with anyone else in one click. The 30-second onboard plays immediately for the new viewer, requiring no account, no app, and no commitment.

To amplify this loop, include a verbal call to action at the end of the onboard: "If you enjoyed this, forward it to someone who would too." Every share is a self-contained marketing asset.

5.2 Pott Cross-Promotion

Joining a Pott (Pillar 3) is the single most effective growth strategy. When 10 creators with 5,000 contacts each cross-promote a weekly Pott feed, every member's content potentially reaches 50,000 people.

The Pott's Curator selects your best .jmpg for the feed; your best work reaches audiences who have never heard of you but trust the Curator's judgment.

5.3 QR-to-Digital Bridge

For creators with a physical presence (restaurants, shops, live events, conferences), QR codes are a growth engine. Every QR scan converts a physical encounter into a digital relationship.

  • A chef places a QR code on the menu → diners watch a .jmpg of the dish being prepared → subscribe for weekly recipes
  • A musician places a QR code on the concert poster → passersby hear a 30-second preview → subscribe for upcoming releases
