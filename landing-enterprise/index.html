Common Questions

What people ask
first.

Does this replace existing governance tools?

No. WINDI adds a verification layer at the approval boundary. Your existing systems continue to generate, recommend, route, and store. WINDI seals the accountable decision event and makes it independently verifiable.

Does this expose sensitive content publicly?

No. The model operates with hash-only evidence and the metadata required for verification. The proof is portable without publishing the underlying content. You control what is exposed.

Why is a public verification endpoint critical?

Because it removes the dependency on trusting the originating interface. The stronger claim is not that the system logged something. The stronger claim is that anyone authorised to inspect can verify the decision state independently — without contacting your organisation.
