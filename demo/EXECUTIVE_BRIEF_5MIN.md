## ⏱️ Minute 1: What is WINDI?

**One sentence:**
> WINDI is a governance layer that makes AI-assisted document processing auditable, compliant, and verifiable.

**Three bullet points:**
- AI processes documents and classifies risk
- Humans approve all governance-relevant decisions
- Every action is sealed in an immutable ledger

**Visual:**
```
  AI → Classification → Human Decision → Ledger Seal
```
