Positioning

A new layer of the internet, built from ideas that already work.

WINDI is a trust verification protocol for institutional documents. It enables any organization to generate governance proofs that any other organization can independently verify — without exchanging sensitive data, without prior integration, and without trusting anyone.

It draws from five decades of proven digital infrastructure, applying the same principles that made the internet reliable — but directed at a new target: the integrity of human decisions and institutional records.

Onboarding

How institutions join the WINDI network

A clear, verifiable path from independent system to recognized participant. As familiar as registering a domain — as consequential as joining a regulated network.

01
Application
The institution requests to join the network and proves its legal identity. A binding between the digital node and the real-world entity is established.
↳ Like requesting a domain name — you declare who you are
02
Verification
The WINDI Hub validates the institution's identity, reviews its governance capacity, and prepares a unique node certificate.
↳ Like a Certificate Authority verifying your organization
03
Certification
The institution receives its cryptographic identity — a signed certificate that binds its name, its keys, and its authorization. From this moment, it can issue verifiable governance proofs.
↳ Like receiving your HTTPS certificate — the world can now verify you
04
First Registration
The institution submits its first governance proof. This event is permanently recorded in the transparency log — the node's first act as a federation participant.
↳ Like your first SWIFT transaction — you're now in the network
05
Recognition
The institution appears on the public Trust List as an ACTIVE node. From that moment, any party in the world can verify that proofs issued by this institution came from a recognized, legitimate source at a specific point in time.
↳ Like appearing in the DNS — resolvable, verifiable, trusted
