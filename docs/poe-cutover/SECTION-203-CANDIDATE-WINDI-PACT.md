## WINDI Pact

The WINDI Pact establishes a scientific and universal agreement defining the basilar principles of diplomacy between intelligent systems.

The Pact separates governance, execution, and analysis; enables cooperation through protocols rather than control; and provides traceability and accountability compatible with existing regulatory frameworks such as ISO/IEC 42001.

The WINDI-PACT governs interaction and coexistence, not intelligence itself.
