### 5.2 Rationale

Black-box model integration without architectural audit violates multiple German and European regulatory requirements:

1. **BaFin MaRisk AT 7.2** requires model validation and understanding of model logic for credit decisions
2. **EU AI Act Art. 13** mandates transparency obligations for high-risk AI systems

4. Side-car API explanations are insufficient — regulators require access to model logic, not post-hoc explanations
