The 5 Layers of Identity

ALMA — Lead Inception

Port :8096 · ID Genesis

The entry point. Leads captured from landing pages are stored in an append-only ledger with hash chain integrity. Never processed without human approval (I1).
1

DID — Wallet Bridge

Lead → Wallet Conversion

When a lead is approved by human, the bridge generates: human_id (UUIDv7), Ed25519 keypair, fingerprint (SHA-256), and wallet_id (WALLET-YYYYMMDD-NNNN).
2

CEREBRO — Wallet Service

Port :8099

The core service that provisions and manages wallets. 10-step provisioning flow: validate, idempotence check, generate keys, insert immutable record, create context, register in Forensic Ledger, link, store keys.
3

Identity Gates

LAW :8122 · Travel :8126

Domain-specific identity systems. LAW Gate for legal professionals, Travel Gate for travelers. Both support Magic Link Login and follow state machine: UNBORN → PROVISIONAL → EMAIL_PENDING → VERIFIED.
4

LEDGER — Forensic Proof

Port :8101

Every significant action is sealed: receipt_id, content_hash, actor, verify_url. Invariant I11: Once sealed = IRREMEDIABLE.
5

MUNDO — Public Verification

/verify-public/

Anyone worldwide can verify: GET /verify-public/?id=RECEIPT-ID. Returns hash, creator wallet_id, timestamp, QR code. No PII exposed. Mathematical proof of existence.
