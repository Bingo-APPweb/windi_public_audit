1. Fundamental Principle

Core Axiom AI Detection within WINDI is classified as INTERPRETATION, not VERIFICATION.

WINDI does not claim truth from AI detection. It classifies uncertainty.

This doctrine establishes the epistemological boundaries of AI-generated content detection within the WINDI constitutional framework. The system operates under the principle that heuristic analysis can indicate probability but cannot establish cryptographic proof.

2. Hierarchy of Truth

WINDI implements a stratified verification system with three distinct levels of confidence:

Level Name Nature Source
1 Guarantee Cryptographic WINDI Forensic Ledger (I11)
2 Mathematical Proof Structural Hash validation, QR extraction
4 Interpretation Heuristic AI pattern detection

Note: Level 3 is reserved for future expansion (e.g., witness attestation).

3. Declared Limitations

The AI Detection layer (Modus 4) explicitly acknowledges the following limitations:

  • Does not prove origin — Cannot cryptographically establish authorship
  • Does not guarantee authenticity — Heuristics indicate probability, not certainty
  • Does not replace human judgment — Final decisions remain with humans (I9)
  • Does not seal analysis results — Analysis ≠ Guarantee (I11 protection)

These limitations are features, not bugs. They prevent overreach and maintain constitutional alignment.

4. Actual Function

The AI Detection layer serves the following constitutional purposes:

  • Pattern Identification — Detects statistical markers of artificial generation
  • Risk Signaling — Flags content for human review when indicators are present
  • Decision Support — Provides structured information for human judgment
  • Trilingual Coverage — Operates across EN, DE, PT linguistic domains

5. Institutional Implications

5.1 For Regulators

WINDI's approach to AI detection complies with emerging EU AI Act requirements by explicitly classifying its limitations. The system does not claim autonomous decision-making authority and maintains human oversight at all classification levels.

5.2 For Financial Institutions

The separation of Guarantee (Level 1) from Interpretation (Level 4) allows institutions to implement tiered document verification policies. Cryptographically sealed documents receive different treatment from heuristically analyzed content.

5.3 For Academic Use

The transparent methodology and declared limitations enable research collaboration and peer review of detection accuracy without overclaiming capabilities.
