1. Executive Summary

WINDI VERIFY introduces a structured approach to media and document assessment, distinguishing between:

  • Cryptographic guarantee (Ledger-based verification)
  • Mathematical or structural validation
  • Heuristic interpretation (AI detection)

AI detection is explicitly classified as an interpretative process, not as a source of truth.

This distinction is designed to align with regulatory expectations regarding transparency, auditability, and limitation of automated claims.

2. Conceptual Framework

The system operates on a hierarchy of epistemic confidence:

Level Classification Nature
1 Guarantee Cryptographic (hash + ledger)
2 Mathematical Proof Structural validation
4 Interpretation Heuristic analysis (AI detection)

Only Level 1 provides verifiable truth claims. Levels 2 and 4 provide supporting information, not final assertions.

3. AI Detection (Modus 4)

AI detection is implemented as a heuristic pattern recognition process. It evaluates:

  • linguistic structures
  • repetition patterns
  • use of generic connectors
  • stylistic consistency indicators

The output is expressed as: none / low / medium / high suspicion

This classification reflects probabilistic indicators, not factual determination.

4. Explainability Layer

To ensure transparency and auditability, each result includes:

  • Detected signals, categorized as:
    • neutral (e.g. formal academic style)
    • risk-associated (e.g. repeated generic connectors)
    • positive (e.g. high lexical diversity)
  • Natural language explanation
  • Interpretation note clarifying limitations

This enables human reviewers to understand the reasoning, assess contextual relevance, and avoid blind reliance on automated output.

5. Explicit Limitations

⚠️ The system explicitly does NOT:

  • assert authorship (human vs AI)
  • provide legal proof of origin
  • replace cryptographic verification mechanisms
  • guarantee correctness of heuristic classification
AI detection results must be treated as decision-support signals, not evidence.

6. Regulatory Alignment

The approach is designed to align with:

🇪🇺 EU AI Act

  • transparency of AI systems
  • explainability of outputs
  • avoidance of misleading claims

🇩🇪 BSI Principles

  • traceability of system behavior
  • verifiability of processes
  • separation of mechanisms

🇩🇪 BaFin Expectations

  • risk-aware design
  • no over-reliance on automation
  • human-in-the-loop support

7. Institutional Implications

The system supports:

  • compliance-oriented document verification workflows
  • media risk assessment without overclaiming certainty
  • integration into regulated environments (banking, audit, legal)

It introduces a key distinction:

Not all outputs of an AI system are equal in evidentiary value.

8. Core Principle

Core Axiom

AI detection is not truth.
It is structured uncertainty.

9. Conclusion

WINDI VERIFY does not attempt to determine absolute truth through AI detection.

Instead, it provides a layered verification framework in which:

  • truth is cryptographically anchored
  • structure is mathematically validated
  • uncertainty is transparently classified

This approach enables responsible use of AI in regulated environments.
